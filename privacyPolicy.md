Thank you for using Libroniq (the "App"), developed by Mustafa Enes Demirkaya ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how your personal data is collected, used, protected, and how it can be deleted when you use our App.

1. Data We Collect
To provide the core functionalities of the App, we collect the following data:

Account Information: When you sign in with Google (via Firebase Auth), we collect your name, email address, and profile picture. For anonymous sign-ins, only an anonymous, device-specific ID is generated.

Library Data: Books you add, reading statuses, personal notes, and uploaded book cover photos. This data is securely stored on Firebase Firestore and Firebase Storage and is strictly private.

Usage Data: In-app interactions (via Firebase Analytics) are collected anonymously to troubleshoot errors and improve the user experience.

2. How We Use Your Data
We use the collected data solely for the following purposes:

To synchronize your personal library across your devices.

To provide highly personalized, AI-generated (Google Gemini / Vertex AI) book recommendations based on your reading habits.

To analyze App performance and resolve technical issues.

3. Third-Party Services
Our App uses the following trusted third-party providers to enhance service quality:

Google Services & Firebase: Authentication, database, storage, and analytics (Auth, Firestore, Storage, Analytics, App Check, Vertex AI).

Search APIs: Google Books API and Open Library API (to retrieve book metadata).

RevenueCat: To manage in-app subscription processes.

During AI analysis, book titles and genres may be transmitted to Vertex AI; however, your personal identifiable information is never directly shared with these services.

4. Data Sharing
Your personal data, library contents, and uploaded photos are entirely private to you. We do not share or sell this data to advertisers, data brokers, or unauthorized third parties under any circumstances.

5. Data Deletion and Retention
We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated personal data, you can use the "Delete Account" button located on the Profile page within the App. Upon completing this action, your library, photos, profile information, and all app data are irreversibly and permanently deleted (hard delete) from our servers. Alternatively, you may contact us via email for any data deletion or privacy requests.

6. Children's Privacy
Libroniq is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

7. Contact Us
If you have any questions regarding our privacy policy or your data, please contact us at apperableapps@gmail.com.
